Comparables ("comps")
Similar homes nearby that sold recently in a normal sale. Avoid foreclosures, very old sales, and very different homes.
Valuing a home
Average the comps' price per sq ft, then multiply by the home's sq ft.
Pricing
Too high: it sits and needs price cuts. Too low: it sells fast but the client loses money.
Commission
Sales: about 4–6% of the price. Rentals: about 10–15% of one year of rent. You then split it with your firm.
