Group Instructions

Play with friends in the same real estate world.

  1. Click Start a New Game and finish setting up your agent.
  2. Once inside the city, find the Team / Group panel near the top-left.
  3. Click Create a team. Your team code will appear in the panel.
  4. Send Code to your friends outside the game or through the "Public City" chat.
  5. Your friends open the same game link, create their agents, enter the code, and click Join team.
Everyone must enter the same team code and click Join team. Creating a team does not automatically add your friends.
